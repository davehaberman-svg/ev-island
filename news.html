News & Developments

The latest from EV Island and the E&N corridor initiative.

May 27, 2026

The Chair Just Opened Up

Steven Guilbeault's resignation as a Liberal MP sends a significant signal — and opens a window. The Carney government will be looking for high-profile clean energy projects to fund. A shovel-ready NZEV corridor initiative on Vancouver Island is exactly the kind of project that fits that need. The window won't stay open long.

May 28, 2026

Letter to Island Corridor Foundation CEO Thomas Bevan

EV Island submitted a detailed proposal to Thomas Bevan, CEO of the Island Corridor Foundation, outlining the NZEV pilot framework, the Halalt First Nation pilot program, and the case for a new direction for the corridor. The letter was copied to 20 stakeholders including the Cowichan Valley MLA, District of North Cowichan council, the Federation of Canadian Municipalities, and the Senior Advisor to the BC Minister of Energy & Climate Solutions.

September 2025

Everything Electric Expo — Vancouver

EV Island set up a booth at Canada's largest public clean energy event, the Everything Electric Expo (September 5–7, 2025) at the Vancouver Convention Centre. The team ran a rail corridor information campaign and returned with a direct connection to the office of Minister Adrian Dix, Minister of Energy & Climate Solutions.

2024

Snaw-Naw-As Environmental Site Assessment Complete

The Snaw-Naw-As First Nation completed a Phase 1 and Phase 2 Environmental Site Assessment on their E&N corridor segment. The report confirmed contamination from creosote-treated ties requires active remediation — establishing the process framework that subsequent Nations can follow.

2023

Snaw-Naw-As Win Landmark BC Court of Appeal Case

The Snaw-Naw-As First Nation won a BC Court of Appeal case returning their portion of the E&N Railway Corridor to First Nations ownership — establishing a landmark legal precedent for other Nations along the route.
