## What is the ICF File?
The **Island Corridor Foundation (ICF)** owns and manages the former E&N Railway corridor on Vancouver Island, BC. Dave is working to advance an alternative vision: instead of expensive rail reinstatement, convert it into a **low-speed NZEV electric road** under BC Motor Vehicle Act Regulations **Section 24.06 — Neighbourhood Zero Emission Vehicles**.

Key background:
- A **Stantec report (Feb 2025)** for the Cowichan Valley Regional District confirmed rail reinstatement is prohibitively expensive with low projected demand.
- The **Snaw-Naw-As (Nanoose) First Nation** won a BC Court of Appeal case in 2023 returning their corridor segment — establishing a landmark legal precedent for First Nations reversion.
- **Thomas Bevan** is the CEO of the Island Corridor Foundation.
- Dave's target: establish a **"Temporary ICF"** by **July 31, 2026**.
- The corridor intersects **14 First Nations territories** and spans 289 km from Victoria to Courtenay across 4 Regional Districts.

## Core Proposal: NZEV Corridor

- Convert the E&N corridor to a **NZEV pathway** under BC Motor Vehicle Act, Section 24.06
- Low-cost, low-speed — achievable with rails in place + timber gap-fill; no highway-grade construction required
- Corridor intersects 14 First Nations territories — replicable framework for each
- **Legal precedent:** Snaw-Naw-As 2023 BC Court of Appeal win — First Nations can reclaim corridor segments
- **Pilot segment:** Halalt First Nation's 1.53 km stretch near Chemainus (~$1.5M)
- **Demo vehicle:** 2025 BYD Shark (TIP to Oct 31, 2026)
- **Mascot:** Dynasty IT #110 (2001, Kelowna-built) — to be leased to program; carries "Right Shade of Green" branding
- **Green IPO concept:** Government-supported investment vehicle for BC residents tied to corridor development — creates public and First Nations buy-in, long-term capital base. Mark Podiasly (FNMPC) is the right person to engage on this.
